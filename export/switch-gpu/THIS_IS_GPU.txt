GT4Recomp Switch — CoreGS / deko3d GPU build (TASK-174)

This NRO never soft-rasters GS batches on the CPU.
- Batches go through WritebackDk (deko3d). Rejected batches are dropped, not DrawBatch'd.
- Present framebuffer is 640x448 (OS scales); no CPU upscale to 1280x720.
- Texture cache survives frame-buffer BITBLTs (FMV / car-select thrash fix).
- Interlaced FFMD menus hold the previous field on odd presents.

Copy gt4recomp.nro + your ISO to sdmc:/switch/gt4recomp/
Check stderr.log for: gpu_draws, gpu_cpu_fallback (~0), gpu_composed / scanouts.
